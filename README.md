# Retail Sales Analysis Project

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data using Python to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and business trends.

The goal is to identify useful business insights and provide recommendations that can help improve business performance.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Dataset
- Total records: 500
- Total columns: 13
- Data includes orders, products, categories, regions, customers, payment methods, revenue, costs, and profit.

## Data Cleaning
- Removed duplicate records.
- Handled missing categorical values.
- Estimated missing quantities using available sales information.
- Converted order dates into datetime format.
- Checked data types and missing values.

## Analysis Performed
1. Overall sales and profit analysis
2. Category performance analysis
3. Regional performance analysis
4. Payment method analysis
5. Customer analysis
6. Product performance analysis
7. Monthly sales trends
8. Weekday sales trends
9. Quantity, pricing, and discount analysis
10. Correlation analysis

## Key Results
- Total Revenue: 48,989.00
- Total Profit: 17,413.78
- Average Profit: 34.83
- Overall Profit Margin: 35.55%

## Key Business Insights
- Home & Kitchen generated the highest category revenue and profit.
- North was the strongest region.
- Cookware Set generated the highest product revenue and profit.
- Wireless Mouse sold the highest quantity.
- Card payments generated the highest total profit.
- New customers contributed slightly more revenue than returning customers.
- July generated the highest monthly revenue and profit.
- Thursday generated the highest weekday revenue and profit.

## Business Recommendations
- Maintain sufficient stock of high-performing products.
- Investigate weaker category and regional performance.
- Encourage repeat purchases through customer loyalty programs.
- Study monthly and weekday sales trends to improve business planning.
- Monitor discounts and costs to protect profit margins.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Python-based data analysis can turn raw retail data into meaningful business insights. It helps identify strong-performing areas, understand business trends, and support better decision-making.
